## 6. Regulatory Classification and Boundary Controls

1. **Evaluation Status:** The production model retains the permanent classification:
   ```
   EXPERIMENTAL_NOT_FIELD_VALIDATED
   ```
2. **Uncalibrated Output Scores:** All scores returned by the model represent raw softmax probabilities. They are not calibrated field confidence estimates.
3. **Prohibition of Medical Claims:** The model is not approved for species-level differentiation (e.g. distinguishing *Anopheles gambiae* from *Anopheles stephensi*) or for detecting pathogens (malaria, dengue, Zika, or chikungunya).
4. **Larvae Isolation:** Larvae submissions route exclusively to the video worker and do not invoke the adult ONNX model.
