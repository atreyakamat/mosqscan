## 1. Executive Summary

This report delivers the structural partition and specimen isolation audit for the extended 8-epoch training pipeline implemented in `scripts/train_extended_combined.py`.

### Primary Audit Findings:
1. **Checkpoint Identification:** All reported epoch validation predictions (Epochs 1–8) and the headline 44/44 global evaluation were generated by a **single model training trajectory**, saved as [`models/vectora_extended_best.pt`](file:///home/nimr/Projects/mosqscan/models/vectora_extended_best.pt) (SHA-256: `b5697a7f177b03988084060de1446eba2e9bc6b0913d2db796b448fbba3d6108`).
2. **Failure to Execute Cross-Validation:** Although `StratifiedGroupKFold(n_splits=5, shuffle=True, random_state=42)` was instantiated, the script invoked `next(...)` exactly once. **Only Fold 1 was ever trained or validated**. Folds 2, 3, 4, and 5 were never executed.
3. **Out-of-Fold Status:**
   - **Epoch Logs (9 specimens):** The 9 validation specimens evaluated during Epochs 1–8 were held-out from the 35 training specimens of that single split.
   - **Headline 44/44 Result:** **Not out-of-fold**. Evaluated on all 44 physical specimens simultaneously using the Fold 1 checkpoint, meaning **35 of the 44 specimens (79.55%) were directly in the training set**.
4. **Specimen Isolation within Fold 1:** Zero data leakage occurred between the training and validation partitions of Fold 1. All 9 validation parent specimens and all 483 of their verified precomputed derivatives were strictly excluded from the training split.

1. **Why the Extended Model is NOT Cross-Validated:** Only 1 of the 5 folds was executed. A model cannot be designated "cross-validated" unless all $K$ folds are trained and evaluated out-of-fold.
2. **Why the 44/44 Score is In-Sample Resubstitution:** When Checkpoint 1 was evaluated on all 44 original photographs, 35 of those specimens were the exact images the network had just trained on across 8 epochs.
